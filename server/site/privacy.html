Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how codwebx ("codwebx", "we", "us", "our"), an IT company based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, collects, uses, stores and protects information. It applies to our company website (codwebx.in) and to our content-publishing application codwebx Reels Studio (reel.codwebx.in).

1. Who we are

codwebx builds websites, apps, custom software and POS systems for businesses. codwebx Reels Studio is an internal tool that we use to create short-form and long-form videos (such as Hindi/Hinglish spiritual-wisdom content) and publish them to our own YouTube and Instagram channels.

Contact: codwebx@gmail.com · +91 75750 97762 · Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Website visitors and enquiries

  • Information you give us: name, email address, phone number, business name and project details that you submit through our enquiry form, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and timestamps, collected automatically in standard server logs for security and diagnostics.

2.2 codwebx Reels Studio (Google / YouTube user data)

Reels Studio is operated by codwebx for publishing our own videos. It is not offered as a public service and we do not collect personal data from members of the public through it. When the codwebx administrator connects a YouTube channel, Google asks for permission through the standard OAuth consent screen. We request only the following scope:

ScopeWhy we need it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.uploadTo upload videos, with their title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publish time, to the connected YouTube channel.

The only Google user data we receive and store is the OAuth refresh token that allows the app to upload videos on behalf of the connected account, plus the ID and URL of videos we upload. We do not read, download, analyse or store your YouTube videos, subscribers, comments, analytics or any other account content.

2.3 Instagram (Meta)

If the administrator connects an Instagram professional account, we store an access token and use it only to publish our own Reels to that account through Meta's official API.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to enquiries, prepare proposals and deliver the services you ask for.
  • To keep our website and systems secure, prevent abuse and fix technical problems.
  • To upload and schedule our own videos on the connected YouTube / Instagram accounts (Reels Studio only).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

codwebx's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing video-upload feature described above.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, except as needed to provide the upload feature, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting or interest-based advertising.
  • No human reads Google user data unless we have your consent for specific data, it is needed for security or abuse investigation, or the law requires it.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models, and we do not send YouTube account data to any AI service.

Our use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Storage, security and retention

  • Data is stored on private cloud servers that we control, in a database that is not publicly accessible. The Reels Studio dashboard is protected by a username and password and all traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and for our business records, then deleted on request or when no longer needed.
  • The YouTube refresh token is kept until the account is disconnected or access is revoked. Server logs are kept for a limited period for security purposes.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not share personal information with third parties except service providers who help us operate (for example hosting, email and messaging providers) under appropriate confidentiality, or where required by law. Reels Studio uses third-party AI and media services (such as Google Gemini, text-to-speech and image-generation providers) to create our own video content from our own scripts and topics; no personal data of visitors or of connected Google accounts is sent to them. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

7. Cookies

Our website and the public pages of Reels Studio do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future we will update this policy.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Revoke Google access: you can remove codwebx's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the account.
  • Access, correction, deletion: email codwebx@gmail.com to ask what data we hold about you or to have it corrected or deleted. We respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

9. Children

Our website and services are directed at businesses and are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International transfers

We are based in India. Our cloud providers may process data in other countries; where this happens we rely on reasonable safeguards.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
