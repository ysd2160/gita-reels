codwebx Reels Studio

codwebx Reels Studio is the video-publishing tool of codwebx, an IT company in Ahmedabad, India. It helps us create Hindi/Hinglish Bhagavad Gita wisdom Shorts, Reels and longer videos, and publish them to our own YouTube and Instagram channels.

Team loginPrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

What this app does

  • Plan & write: builds a content calendar and writes short scripts with verse references and explanations.
  • Produce: generates narration, visuals and captions and renders vertical (9:16) and long-form videos.
  • Review & approve: a codwebx team member reviews every video before it is published.
  • Publish & schedule: uploads approved videos, with title, description and tags, to codwebx's own YouTube channel (and Instagram), immediately or at a scheduled time.

How we use Google / YouTube access

When a codwebx administrator connects the channel, the app asks for a single permission, youtube.upload, solely to upload videos to that channel. We do not read or store your YouTube videos, comments, subscribers or analytics, and we do not sell or share Google user data. Details are in our Privacy Policy. codwebx's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can use it

Reels Studio is an internal tool used by codwebx staff. The dashboard is password protected; this public page and our policies are open to everyone.
